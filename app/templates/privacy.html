UhOhCustoms Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Welcome to UhOhCustoms! This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information when you use our web application. UhOhCustoms is a third-party developer application with production approval from Riot Games, allowing players to access their custom League of Legends data through Riot’s Single Sign-On (SSO) and Application Programming Interfaces (API).

By using UhOhCustoms, you consent to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

  • Riot Account Information: Your Riot ID, summoner name, and account region.
  • Game Data: Custom game match history, statistics, and related metadata.
  • Technical Data: IP addresses, browser type, operating system, and interaction logs to enhance security and functionality.

We do not collect or store excessive data about you or your account beyond what is absolutely necessary for our services.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Authenticate your access via Riot’s SSO.
  • Retrieve and display your custom game data.
  • Detect and prevent fraudulent activities or abuse.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal data to third parties. However, we may share information in the following cases:

  • With Riot Games: As required under Riot’s API policies for auditing and compliance.
  • Legal Obligations: If required by law, regulation, or legal process.
  • Security Purposes: To investigate potential violations or protect the integrity of our services.

5. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data, including encryption and secure access controls. User-related data is only stored for as long as necessary to provide our services.

6. Your Rights and Choices

  • Access and Review: You can view the data retrieved from Riot’s API via UhOhCustoms.
  • Data Deletion: If you wish to remove your data, you can request deletion via our support contact.
  • Opt-Out: If you no longer want UhOhCustoms to access your Riot account data, you can revoke access through your Riot account settings.

7. Cookies and Tracking

UhOhCustoms may use cookies or similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience. These may be used for:

  • Session management.

You can manage or disable cookies via your browser settings.

8. Third-Party Links

Our site may contain links to third-party sites or services. We are not responsible for their privacy practices and encourage you to review their policies separately.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as needed. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of UhOhCustoms after such changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at:
